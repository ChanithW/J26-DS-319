🌟 Flare Detection and Energy Classification

Stars sometimes release sudden bursts of energy called flares, and these can affect whether nearby planets could support life . This component uses NASA's TESS telescope data 🔭 and a deep-learning model to find flares in a star's brightness record and tell how powerful each one is (C, M, X or Superflare), both in a single step. It is especially good at catching weak flares that existing methods often miss 🔍, and its results are passed on to the rest of the project to help assess planetary habitability.
